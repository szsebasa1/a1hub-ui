Writing Rules
Do
  • Open with a real observation, tension, or lesson
  • Use "we" for solutions and "you" for goals
  • Anchor claims in real data (98% retention, 7-day hire time)
  • Lead with outcomes, then add specifics
  • Use active voice: say who does what
  • Adapt depth to audience: technical vs. executive
  • Be warm and direct at the same time
Do Not
  • Use em dashes
  • Say "game-changing," "revolutionary," "leverage," or "synergy"
  • Open with "In today's fast-paced world..."
  • Write dense paragraphs without clear structure
  • Overclaim AI outcomes without acknowledging tradeoffs
  • Write thought leadership that could belong to any company
  • Use humor or idioms that don't translate across cultures
